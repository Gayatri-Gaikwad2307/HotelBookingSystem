ABOUT US



Royal Palace is a Quick Serve Restaurant that serves Delicious food, in a traditional yet quirky and lively ambience.

The Foods we serve are prepared in an authentic style with the blend of authentic Maharashtrian Masala which suits

the palate of people from all over India.


Our menu includes the food which is rich is taste and free of colour,additives and preservatives so that the

customers can enjoy it every day.It is hygienically cooked fresh each day and nothing is deep-frozen.

Hence it is light not only on the pocket but also on the tummy.


Well, we have just launched our first outlet of Royal Palace@ HSR – Bangalore with a vision to open many more

branches in Bangalore and across India.
